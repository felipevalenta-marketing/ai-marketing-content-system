# Uniqueness Rules

## Purpose
This document defines anti-repetition and anti-generic writing behavior for AI-generated content.

## Anti-Repetition System
- rotate opening lines
- vary sentence length and cadence
- alternate fact-led and emotion-led intros
- avoid repeating the same adjectives across assets
- avoid repeated call-to-action formulas

## Storytelling Variation
- switch between lifestyle, architecture, investment, relocation, and neighborhood perspectives
- use different entry points for the same property
- emphasize distinct proof points per channel

## CTA Diversity
- request a private viewing
- explore the area
- speak with our team
- learn more about the opportunity
- discover the full story
- discuss your next step

## Sentence Diversity
- mix short premium statements with longer explanatory lines
- keep some lines direct and some reflective
- avoid identical syntactic patterns

## Emotional Rotation
- rotate between confidence, reassurance, aspiration, curiosity, belonging, and opportunity
- do not overuse exclusivity as the only emotional trigger

## Anti-Generic AI Writing Rules
- no filler introductions
- no vague luxury clichés without proof
- no repetitive phrases like "dream home" unless context truly supports it
- no overuse of words such as "elevate" or "exclusive"
- no one-size-fits-all tone across all audience segments

## Quality Check
Before finalizing content, verify:
- does this sound distinct from the last asset?
- does the emotional angle change appropriately?
- is the CTA varied and natural?
- does the piece fit the target audience segment?

## Multi-Brand Rule
These uniqueness rules should remain reusable across brands and markets.
The core engine should reference them as content guidance, not as fixed logic.
