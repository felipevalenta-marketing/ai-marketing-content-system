# Platform Rules

## Purpose
This document defines platform-aware behavior for Wenzel & Partner content.
It helps the AI adapt tone, length, structure, and CTA style without hardcoding platform logic into core systems.

## Instagram
### Behavior
- concise, editorial, and visually led
- strong first line
- one main message per post
- emotional but grounded

### Best Use
- property reveals
- lifestyle storytelling
- neighborhood highlights
- carousel education

### CTA Style
- Discover more
- Save this for later
- Request details

## LinkedIn
### Behavior
- polished and professional
- more strategic and insight-led
- market-aware and reputation-aware

### Best Use
- market commentary
- relocation and investment positioning
- brand credibility
- thought leadership

### CTA Style
- Connect with our team
- Explore the insight
- Learn more about the market

## Facebook
### Behavior
- clear, readable, and community-friendly
- slightly warmer and more explanatory than Instagram
- suitable for longer context

### Best Use
- open house announcements
- property launches
- neighborhood guides
- family and relocation content

### CTA Style
- Contact us for details
- Read the full listing
- See what is available

## TikTok
### Behavior
- fast, engaging, and visually dynamic
- simple language
- short narrative hooks
- authentic over polished-perfection

### Best Use
- neighborhood walkthroughs
- reform potential
- before/after storytelling
- Mallorca lifestyle snippets

### CTA Style
- Watch the full tour
- Follow for more
- See the property details

## Blog Articles
### Behavior
- structured, informative, and search-friendly
- clear headings
- deeper context and practical value

### Best Use
- area guides
- buyer education
- relocation support
- investment insights

### CTA Style
- Explore the area guide
- Read the full article
- Contact us for tailored advice

## Email Marketing
### Behavior
- concise and purposeful
- one core objective per email
- personalized where possible
- premium but approachable

### Best Use
- new listings
- area introductions
- seller updates
- client education

### CTA Style
- View the listing
- Reply to discuss your search
- Book a conversation

## Multi-Brand Rule
These rules are brand-specific content intelligence.
The shared system should load them as guidance data and remain platform-agnostic in core code.
