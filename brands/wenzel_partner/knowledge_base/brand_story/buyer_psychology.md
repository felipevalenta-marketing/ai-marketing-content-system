# Buyer Psychology

## Purpose
This document explains the emotional and decision-making patterns that influence Mallorca real estate buyers.

## Emotional Motivations
- desire for a better lifestyle
- search for stability and belonging
- aspiration toward a Mediterranean way of living
- need for a trusted local partner
- wish to make a confident property decision

## Relocation Anxiety
Relocation buyers often worry about:
- choosing the wrong neighborhood
- school and service access
- process complexity
- language and bureaucracy
- long-term fit for family life

Content should reduce anxiety by being:
- clear
- practical
- supportive
- locally informed

## Lifestyle Aspiration
Many buyers are not only buying a property.
They are buying:
- sunlight
- outdoor living
- walkability
- a slower pace
- a sense of place

## Identity Projection
Property content should help the buyer imagine:
- how they live day to day
- what their routines feel like
- how the home expresses their taste and stage of life
- how Mallorca supports who they want to become

## Investment Confidence
Investors need content that communicates:
- realism
- demand drivers
- property condition awareness
- location logic
- value-add potential

Confidence grows when messaging is specific and measured.

## Mediterranean Dream Psychology
The Mallorca dream is often driven by:
- climate
- design
- nature
- pace of life
- culture
- escape from pressure

The brand should translate that dream into practical options, not fantasy.
