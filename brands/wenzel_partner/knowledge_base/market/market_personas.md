# Market Personas

## Purpose
This document captures recurring buyer and seller personas in the Mallorca market.
It is designed for AI-readable prompt grounding and reusable campaign strategy.

## Persona 1: German Buyers
### Motivations
- secure a trusted property purchase in Mallorca
- find quality, value, and stability
- access a familiar process with strong local support
- balance lifestyle and investment logic

### Concerns
- legal and process clarity
- language barriers
- market transparency
- reliable after-sale support

### Preferred Areas
- Palma Old Town
- Son Vida
- Santa Catalina
- Portixol
- Soller

### Emotional Drivers
- security
- confidence
- control
- long-term planning
