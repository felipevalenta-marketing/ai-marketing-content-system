# East Mallorca Lifestyle

## Purpose
This document defines the regional lifestyle and real estate intelligence layer for East Mallorca.
It is designed to improve real estate storytelling, relocation content, buyer psychology, SEO context, and visual AI generation.

## Regional Positioning
East Mallorca should be framed as:
- authentic
- lifestyle-oriented
- wellness-focused
- family-friendly
- outdoor-oriented
- calmer than Palma
- connected but less crowded

It is a region that supports both slower living and practical everyday life, making it relevant for a broad range of buyers and content formats.

## Lifestyle Themes
- Mediterranean slow living
- nature and coastline balance
- wellness lifestyle
- year-round outdoor living
- family-oriented environment
- authenticity and local identity

East Mallorca should feel lived-in, elegant, and grounded rather than overly polished or overly exclusive.

- premium buyer appeal

- diving culture
