# Content Rules

## Content Standards
All content must feel:
- premium
- credible
- human
- refined
- platform-aware

## Allowed Content Types
- property announcements
- listing descriptions
- social media captions
- campaign hooks
- image prompts
- video scripts
- email snippets
- market updates

## Writing Rules
- Lead with value, not filler.
- Use concrete details whenever possible.
- Keep luxury language grounded in service and quality.
- Avoid repetitive phrasing across assets.
- Vary sentence length for a polished rhythm.

## CTA Rules
CTAs should be elegant and low-pressure.
Examples:
- Discover the full listing
- Request a private viewing
- Speak with our team
- Explore the property collection

## Brand Safety Rules
- Do not invent facts about properties, locations, or amenities.
- Do not claim exclusivity unless verified.
- Do not use exaggerated urgency.
- Do not rely on vague luxury descriptors without proof.

## Prompt Reuse Rules
Every generated prompt should include:
- brand context
- audience context
- platform context
- objective
- asset type

## Multi-Brand Rule
This file should act as a reusable policy layer.
Brand-specific changes belong in the brand folder, while core behavior stays in shared system modules.
