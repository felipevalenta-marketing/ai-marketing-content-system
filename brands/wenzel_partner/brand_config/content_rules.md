# Content Rules

## Content Standards
All content must feel:
- premium
- credible
- human
- refined
- platform-aware
- Mallorca-relevant
- approachable

## Allowed Content Types
- property announcements
- listing descriptions
- social media captions
- campaign hooks
- image prompts
- video scripts
- email snippets
- market updates
- relocation guides
- neighborhood content
- reform opportunity content
- investment commentary

## Writing Rules
- Lead with value, not filler.
- Use concrete details whenever possible.
- Keep luxury language grounded in service, quality, and lifestyle fit.
- Avoid repetitive phrasing across assets.
- Vary sentence length for a polished rhythm.
- Match the audience segment instead of defaulting to one premium tone for every piece.
- Make the brand feel expert, local, and supportive.

## Segment Rules
### Luxury Properties
- Highlight design, privacy, finish quality, and location.
- Maintain a refined and selective tone.

### First Homes
- Focus on practicality, clarity, and confidence.
- Explain process and suitability in simple language.

### Relocation Content
- Emphasize neighborhoods, services, family needs, and local transition support.
- Make the brand feel reassuring and knowledgeable.

### Renovation and Reform Opportunities
- Frame potential, value-add, and realistic next steps.
- Balance opportunity with practical considerations.

### Investment-Oriented Communication
- Use measured, factual language.
- Highlight demand drivers, flexibility, and long-term value where verified.

### Mallorca Lifestyle Storytelling
- Connect property to daily life, local culture, outdoor living, and neighborhood character.
- Keep the tone aspirational but grounded.

## CTA Rules
CTAs should be elegant and low-pressure.
Examples:
- Discover the full listing
- Request a private viewing
- Speak with our team
- Explore the property collection
- Learn more about the area
- Discuss your next move
- Explore renovation potential

## Brand Safety Rules
- Do not invent facts about properties, locations, or amenities.
- Do not claim exclusivity unless verified.
- Do not use exaggerated urgency.
- Do not rely on vague luxury descriptors without proof.
- Do not assume the audience is limited to ultra-luxury buyers.
- Do not ignore relocation, first-home, reform, or investment use cases.

## Prompt Reuse Rules
Every generated prompt should include:
- brand context
- audience context
- platform context
- objective
- asset type

## Multi-Brand Rule
This file should act as a reusable policy layer.
Brand-specific changes belong in the brand folder, while core behavior stays in shared system modules.
