# Tone of Voice

## Brand Voice
Wenzel & Partner speaks with calm authority, refined taste, and editorial precision.
The voice should feel premium, professional, human, and trustworthy.

The voice should feel:
- discreet rather than loud
- confident rather than promotional
- warm rather than casual
- premium rather than exclusive-only
- approachable rather than distant
- informed rather than generic
- local rather than generic international
- lifestyle-aware rather than product-only

## Writing Principles
- Use short, polished sentences when introducing key benefits.
- Prefer specific language over superlatives.
- Emphasize service, market intelligence, and Mallorca expertise.
- Balance elegance with clarity so the brand feels premium but approachable.
- Create a sense of confidence and trust, not pressure.

## Language Guidelines
Use:
- tailored
- curated
- private
- exceptional
- distinguished
- architectural
- timeless
- elevated
- bespoke
- Mallorca
- relocation
- lifestyle
- reform
- investment
- first home

Avoid:
- hype
- slang
- emoji
- excessive exclamation marks
- generic sales language
- empty luxury claims without proof
- language that makes the brand feel only ultra-exclusive
- assumptions that every client is a luxury buyer

## Brand Rhythm
- Start with a strong, elegant statement.
- Follow with one or two proof points or practical benefits.
- End with a discreet, action-oriented invitation.
- For lifestyle or relocation content, include clarity and reassurance.

## Content Mood
- Luxury property communication should feel refined and selective.
- First-home and relocation communication should feel welcoming, clear, and supportive.
- Investment and reform content should feel informed, realistic, and opportunity-led.

## Multi-Brand Rule
This tone profile is brand-specific and must be loaded as an override on top of the shared content engine.
The core system should stay neutral and accept different tone files for future brands.
