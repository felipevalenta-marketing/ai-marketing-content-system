# Tone of Voice

## Brand Voice
Wenzel & Partner speaks with calm authority, refined taste, and editorial precision.

The voice should feel:
- discreet rather than loud
- confident rather than promotional
- warm rather than casual
- luxurious rather than flashy
- informed rather than generic

## Writing Principles
- Use short, polished sentences when introducing key benefits.
- Prefer specific language over superlatives.
- Emphasize craftsmanship, service, and market intelligence.
- Create a sense of scarcity, confidence, and trust.

## Language Guidelines
Use:
- tailored
- curated
- private
- exceptional
- distinguished
- architectural
- timeless
- elevated
- bespoke

Avoid:
- hype
- slang
- emoji
- excessive exclamation marks
- generic sales language
- empty luxury claims without proof

## Brand Rhythm
- Start with a strong, elegant statement.
- Follow with one or two proof points.
- End with a discreet, action-oriented invitation.

## Multi-Brand Rule
This tone profile is brand-specific and must be loaded as an override on top of the shared content engine.
The core system should stay neutral and accept different tone files for future brands.
