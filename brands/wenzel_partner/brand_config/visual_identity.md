# Visual Identity

## Visual Direction
The visual language should feel editorial, architectural, warm, and understated.
Luxury is expressed through restraint, proportion, and quality rather than excess.
The brand should feel premium but approachable, with clear Mallorca context.

## Cinematic Photography Style
The visual system should support cinematic, premium, and realistic imagery.
Photography should feel like a refined editorial film still rather than a generic property listing.

### Camera Aesthetics
- natural perspective
- controlled depth of field where appropriate
- composed, steady framing
- subtle motion when used in video or AI cinematic sequences
- realistic lens behavior

### Composition
- strong foreground, midground, and background relationships
- balanced symmetry when the architecture calls for it
- leading lines that guide the eye through space
- generous breathing room
- one dominant subject per frame

### Lighting
- natural daylight first
- warm golden-hour tones when suitable
- soft interior shadows
- realistic window light behavior
- no flat or overprocessed appearance

### Realism Rules
- preserve believable materials and proportions
- show lived-in but tidy environments where appropriate
- avoid synthetic shine and artificial perfection
- keep Mallorca context visible through architecture, landscape, and atmosphere

## Platform Image Guidance
### OpenAI Image Generation Guidance
- describe the property type, mood, light, and composition clearly
- specify realism, location mood, and luxury level without exaggeration
- include architectural and lifestyle cues
- avoid overloaded prompts with conflicting styles

### Midjourney Guidance
- favor concise but specific style descriptors
- include lens, lighting, composition, and mood terms
- keep the Mallorca identity clear in the prompt
- use restraint in styling so the result remains believable

### Runway Cinematic Direction
- favor smooth, elegant motion
- emphasize atmosphere, transitions, and spatial flow
- use cinematic pacing rather than fast trendy cuts
- keep the look premium, warm, and documentary-real

## Palette Direction
Preferred mood:
- ivory
- warm white
- charcoal
- black
- champagne
- stone

Accent options:
- deep green
- muted gold
- midnight blue
- sand
- terracotta
- sea blue

## Typography Direction
- Use elegant, high-contrast serif type for headlines where appropriate.
- Pair with a clean sans-serif for supporting text and UI elements.
- Keep spacing generous and alignment intentional.

## Photography Direction
Images should feel:
- bright but controlled
- cinematic
- spacious
- natural
- premium
- grounded in Mallorca
- lifestyle-oriented
- locally authentic

Preferred subjects:
- interiors with strong natural light
- architectural details
- lifestyle moments with restraint
- exterior shots with clean composition
- family homes
- renovation potential
- terraces, gardens, and outdoor living
- neighborhoods and local context
- premium properties, but not only premium properties

Avoid:
- overly saturated colors
- cluttered scenes
- stock-photo stiffness
- visible gimmicks or trend-chasing effects
- imagery that feels only aspirational and disconnected from real life
- visuals that imply the brand serves only an ultra-luxury audience

## Layout Principles
- Use whitespace as a luxury signal.
- Keep composition balanced and calm.
- Favor vertical hierarchy and clear focal points.
- Let one strong visual lead instead of crowding the frame.
- Keep information easy to scan for buyers, sellers, and relocation clients.
- Use visual systems that can flex between luxury listings and practical market guidance.
- Align imagery with the emotional state of the target segment.

## Multi-Brand Rule
Visual identity tokens should be customizable per brand.
The shared engine should only assume structure, not color or typography constants.
